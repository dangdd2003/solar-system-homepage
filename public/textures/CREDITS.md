# Texture Credits

Planet, Sun, and Moon textures in `planets/` are derived from the **2K** maps from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), distributed
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Attribution: textures by Solar System Scope (www.solarsystemscope.com).

You may use, adapt, and share these textures for any purpose, including
commercially, provided the attribution above is preserved.

The shipped files are WebP re-encodings, downsampled from the 2K source maps
to keep page weight low. Most maps ship at 1024×512 (half the nominal 2K
2048×1024 size); Uranus and Neptune ship at 512×256. The Saturn ring strip
ships at 2048×125.
